What is a SAFE? — A SAFE (Simple Agreement for Future Equity) is a loan-free investment note. The investor gives you money now and gets shares later when you raise a priced round. Add one SAFE per investor or tranche.

What is a Priced Round? — A priced round (Series A, B…) is when investors buy shares at a fixed price. This is also when all your SAFEs convert to equity. Enter the pre-money valuation and the new investment amount.
